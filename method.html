The Framework Method

A clear, repeatable system.

Every client moves through the same six stages, in the same order. Nothing gets designed before it's been defined, and nothing gets built before it's been designed.

01

Stage 1: Discover

Understanding your business, market, and goals.

We start by listening. Through structured conversations and research, we learn how your business works today, who you serve, and what success looks like. This stage removes assumptions before any design work begins.

OutputA clear picture of your business, audience, and objectives
02

Stage 2: Define

Turning insight into strategy.

We translate what we've learned into a defined strategy — positioning, messaging, and a plan for how your brand and business should be structured. This becomes the reference point for every decision that follows.

OutputA documented strategy and brand positioning
03

Stage 3: Design

Giving the strategy a visual identity.

Strategy becomes visible. We design your name (if needed), logo, identity system, and website — always tied back to the definition stage, never designed in isolation.

OutputA complete brand identity and website design
04

Stage 4: Build

Bringing the design to life.

We build what's been designed — a functioning website, brand assets ready for use, and the systems your business needs to operate. Every element is built to be structurally sound, not just visually finished.

OutputA live, functioning website and brand system
05

Stage 5: Launch

Putting your business in front of the world.

We launch your website and brand publicly, with the digital presence, SEO foundation, and business systems needed to operate professionally from day one.

OutputA live business presence, ready for customers
06

Stage 6: Grow

Supporting your business after launch.

A framework isn't finished at launch. We stay involved — refining performance, expanding systems, and advising as your business grows.

OutputOngoing support and continued growth
