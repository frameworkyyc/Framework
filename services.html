Services

What Framework builds.

Four services for businesses of every kind. Each one starts with the problem it solves and ends with the specific things we deliver.

01

Website Design & Development

The problem

A website that looks fine but doesn't say what you do, loads slowly on a phone, or gives visitors no clear next step.

How we solve it

We design and build it around your business and your customers, then set it up properly for search from the first day.

Website design and development
What you get
  • Custom design and development, responsive from the start
  • Performance tuning for fast loads on a phone
  • Technical SEO at launch: structure, titles, structured data, sitemap
  • Hosting and deployment on Cloudflare, where it suits the project
  • An enquiry form that reaches you
02

SEO & Digital Marketing

The problem

Customers search for what you do and find someone else. Your details are inconsistent, and you can't tell whether anything is working.

How we solve it

We start from facts, fix what stops search engines finding you, and measure what changes. No rankings are promised.

SEO and digital marketing in Calgary
What you get
  • A free visibility assessment to start
  • Technical and on-page SEO
  • Local search work and Google Business Profile, where eligible
  • Measurement with tools such as Google Search Console
03

Branding & Identity

The problem

A business that looks different on the truck, the website and the invoice, or looks like everyone else in its category.

How we solve it

We start with strategy, then design an identity and the rules for using it, so everything you publish looks like the same business.

Branding and identity
What you get
  • Logo design or refinement
  • Visual identity: colour, typography and graphic language
  • Brand guidelines and design systems
  • Messaging and voice, where the scope includes it
04

Business Systems & Automation

The problem

Work held together by spreadsheets, email threads and the same details typed in twice.

How we solve it

We build custom web applications that fit how your business already works, so information lives in one place and routine steps run themselves.

Business systems and automation
What you get
  • Custom web applications
  • Client portals
  • Internal dashboards
  • Business workflows and notifications
  • Integrations with services you already use, scoped to the project
