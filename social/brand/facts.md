## Services (services.html): four services, each framed problem → solution → deliverables
1. **Website Design & Development.** Problem: a site that doesn't say what you do, loads slowly on a phone, or gives no clear next step. Delivers custom responsive design and build, phone performance tuning, technical SEO at launch (structure, titles, structured data, sitemap), hosting on Cloudflare where it suits, an enquiry form that reaches you.
2. **SEO & Digital Marketing.** Problem: customers search and find someone else; details are inconsistent; no way to tell what works. Starts from facts, fixes what stops search engines finding you, measures what changes. **No rankings are promised.** Delivers a free visibility assessment, technical and on-page SEO, local search and Google Business Profile where eligible, measurement (e.g. Google Search Console). Broader digital marketing beyond search is **not offered yet**: don't promote it.
3. **Branding & Identity.** Problem: a business that looks different on the truck, the website and the invoice, or like everyone else in its category. Strategy first, then identity and the rules for using it. Delivers logo design or refinement, visual identity, brand guidelines and design systems, messaging and voice where in scope.
4. **Business Systems & Automation.** Problem: work held together by spreadsheets, email threads and double entry. Custom web applications, client portals, internal dashboards, workflows and notifications, integrations assessed per project.
- Strategy is part of every project, not a separate purchase.

## The Framework Method (method.html): six stages, same order, every client
01 Discover (understand business, market, goals) · 02 Define (strategy, positioning, messaging) · 03 Design (identity and website, tied to the definition) · 04 Build (website, brand assets, systems, built structurally sound) · 05 Launch (public launch with SEO foundation and systems) · 06 Grow (stay involved after launch).
Lines from the site: "Nothing gets designed before it's been defined, and nothing gets built before it's been designed." "Drawn, then built." "A framework isn't finished at launch."
