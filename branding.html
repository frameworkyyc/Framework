Why it matters

A brand is why they call you instead of the next one.

Customers judge a contractor before they speak to one: the truck, the sign, the quote, the website. A consistent identity says the business is organised and the work will be too. We build that from what you do, who you serve and what sets you apart.

What you receive

An identity, and the rules to use it.

01

Logo design and refinement

A new mark, or a considered refinement of the one you have, designed to work small and large, in colour and in one colour.

02

Visual identity

Colour, typography and graphic language, with clear rules for how they are used.

03

Brand guidelines and design systems

One reference for the rules and building blocks, so everything you publish stays consistent and anyone you hire applies it correctly.

04

Additional deliverables

Additional deliverables are determined by the scope of the project, for example messaging and voice.

Questions

Questions we get.

Do I need a brand before a website?

No. If you have one we build around it. If you don't, branding can be part of the same project.

Can you refine the logo I already have?

Yes. Logo design and refinement are part of what we do.

What do I receive at the end?

It depends on the project. A logo, a visual identity and brand guidelines are the core, and additional deliverables are determined by the scope of the project.

How long does it take?

Every project is scoped individually, so we don't publish a standard turnaround.

Can you work with a designer I already have?

Yes. For Aurora Surveys we built around the notes, palettes and approach their own designer supplied.
