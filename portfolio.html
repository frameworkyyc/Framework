Featured case study Geomatics engineering — British Columbia & the Canadian North

Aurora Surveys

A website that shows the survey work instead of describing it.

Aurora Surveys provides geomatics engineering and reality capture for transportation, hydroelectric, mining, and energy projects — from downtown Vancouver to Baffin Island.

Aurora supplied their own logo and identity. We designed and built the website around it: a 3D total station walks through a field setup, a traverse map plots each project by latitude, and an animated point cloud shows how ten million raw returns become a usable deliverable.

Case study Professional Services — Canadian project delivery

Red E Corp.

A brand built on day one for a company built to expand — and the website that puts it to work.

Red E Corp. delivers consulting, project management, and project planning for industrial, commercial, and institutional clients. Its founder came up as a carpenter, ironworker, and millwright before running projects from the office.

We started with strategy: who Red E serves, what sets it apart, and the promise behind the name — Red E 4 Anything. The crest, colour, type, voice, and application rules all follow from that, documented in a single brand system.

We then designed and built the website from scratch: a full-screen crest landing page, service pages, projects, a gallery, and a simple way to start a conversation. The client supplied the photography; we edited every image to suit the brand.

Case study Hospitality — Anahim Lake, British Columbia

Eagle's Nest Resort

A complete website — and the photography on it — for a ten-acre lakeside resort in the West Chilcotin.

Eagle's Nest sits on Anahim Lake, one kilometre off Highway 20 — suites, rooms, and log cabins, run by the owners, Diane and Murray.

We built the site around the questions guests actually ask: where it is, how to get there, and which room fits. Every suite and cabin has its own page, and booking is one tap away on every screen.

We also photographed the property on site — the lodge, the cabins, and the waterfront — so every image on the site shows the real place.
