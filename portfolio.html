Featured case study Professional Services — Canadian project delivery

Red E Corp.

A brand built on day one for a company built to expand.

Red E Corp. delivers consulting, project management, and project planning for industrial, commercial, and institutional clients. Its founder came up as a carpenter, ironworker, and millwright before running projects from the office.

We started with strategy: who Red E serves, what sets it apart, and the promise behind the name — Red E 4 Anything. The crest, colour, type, voice, and application rules all follow from that, documented in a single brand system.

Case study Hospitality — Anahim Lake, British Columbia

Eagle's Nest Resort

A complete website for a ten-acre lakeside resort in the West Chilcotin.

Eagle's Nest sits on Anahim Lake, one kilometre off Highway 20 — suites, rooms, and log cabins, run by the owners, Diane and Murray.

We built the site around the questions guests actually ask: where it is, how to get there, and which room fits. Every suite and cabin has its own page and photographs, and booking is one tap away on every screen.
