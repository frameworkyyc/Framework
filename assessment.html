02The problem

Having a website doesn’t mean people can find it.

Customers often search for what a company does and where it works, not for its name. Someone looking for a concrete contractor, an electrician or a renovation company in their city may never type your business name at all.

Whether your business is easy to find, easy to understand and easy to trust in that moment depends on more than how the website looks. How the site is structured, whether your services are described in plain language, how clearly you say where you work, how complete and consistent your public business profiles are, and the credibility signals you show — projects, testimonials, qualifications — can all play a part.

This matters for construction companies, contractors and trades, whose customers often search by service and location. It applies just as much to any other service-based business.
