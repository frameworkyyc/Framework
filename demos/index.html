Common questions.

Will the website get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can promise a ranking, and we won’t. What we do is build the foundations search engines look for: a clear structure, pages that say what you do and where you work, fast pages on a phone, and guidance on your Google Business Profile.

How much does a contractor website cost?

It depends on what your business needs, so we scope each project instead of quoting from a price list. A short conversation, or the free assessment, is the quickest way to learn what you’d need now and what can wait.

What do I need to get started?

A conversation. Tell us what you do, where you work and what kind of jobs you want more of. Photos of past work help a great deal, and if you don’t have good ones yet we can talk about that too.

Do you stay involved after the website launches?

Yes. A website isn’t finished at launch. We stay involved to refine it and to build on it as your business grows.

Privacy: what we record

So Framework can see which styles are popular, we keep simple, private counts. No cookies, no account and no email address.

  • Which tracking link brought you here, if you used one. That shows which link was opened, not who you are.
  • The discipline, vibe and colour you choose.
  • A company name only if you press “See your name on the website”.
  • Whether you open the full demo or send an enquiry.
  • Your rough country and device type. Never your IP address, browser details, email or the contents of an enquiry.

Kept for up to 180 days and visible only to Framework staff. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is recorded, not even a link visit. See the privacy policy.
