How much does a kitchen cost?

Most of our kitchens fall between $35,000 and $90,000 depending on size, cabinetry and layout changes. Your quote is fixed before we start.

Can we live at home during the work?

Usually, yes. We set up dust barriers and keep a working path through the house.

Who handles permits?

We do. Permits, inspections and engineering are all included in your contract.

How soon can you start?

Design typically starts within 2 weeks; construction 4–8 weeks after that depending on materials.
