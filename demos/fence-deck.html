How long does a new fence take?

Most residential fences are installed in 1–3 days once permits and utility locates are complete, usually 1–2 weeks from your quote.

Do I need a permit?

In Calgary most fences under 2 m don't need one, but decks over 0.6 m do. We check your specific yard and handle anything required.

Cedar or composite?

Cedar costs less up front and looks classic; composite costs more but needs almost no maintenance. We'll price both so you can compare.

Do you work in winter?

Yes. We install fences year-round using ground-thaw equipment, and book deck builds from April to November.
