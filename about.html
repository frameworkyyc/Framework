The founder

From the job site to the browser.

Where I came from, and why Framework exists.

01

The trades

I trained in General Carpentry at St. Clair College and went straight to work. Over the next decade I put in more than 10,000 hours across residential, commercial, industrial and oil and gas projects: as a carpenter, a scaffolder, a heavy equipment operator and a structural steel welder.

02

What the field teaches you

A job site doesn't care about good intentions. Foundations go in before walls go up. Someone has to coordinate the trades, and the schedule is the plan. Skip a step and you pay for it later, usually in front of the client. You learn to plan the work, then work the plan.

Strategy first. Design second. Execution third. It is how a structure gets built, and it is how a business should be.

03

Why Framework exists

When I looked at how most small businesses build their online presence, I saw the opposite of a well-run job: a logo first, a website bolted on, nothing connected, and nobody coordinating it. Framework exists to do it the way a job should be done. Plan first, build on a foundation, check the work, and hand it over properly.

It is also why I specialize in contractors and trades. I know what a contractor needs from a website, because I have been the one on the job.
