Privacy

Privacy notice.

What Framework collects when you request a Free Online Presence Assessment, why, and how to reach us about it.

Who we are

Framework is a business strategy, brand and website studio based in Calgary, Alberta. You can reach us about this notice or your information at hello@frameworkco.ca.

What we collect

When you request an assessment on our website, we collect what you enter in the form:

  • Your business name, website address, city and province, and type of business.
  • Your name and business email address, and your phone number if you provide one.
  • Optional details you choose to add: services, service area, social media or business profile links, your main concern, and any other information.
  • Whether you chose to receive occasional marketing emails (this box is unticked by default).

We also record the date and time of your request and, if you arrived from one of our ads or links, the campaign details in that link (for example the ad campaign name) and the page you came from. We keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address to limit spam; we do not store the address itself.

How we use it

We use this information to research your business using publicly available information, prepare your personalized assessment, email it to you, and contact you about it. We do not use your request to add you to a marketing list. We send marketing emails only if you ticked the optional box.

Who handles it

Requests are stored in our account with Cloudflare, which hosts this website, and are emailed to our team through an email delivery service we use to send the notification and your confirmation email. We do not sell your information.

Advertising measurement

If we are running Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, this page may offer to turn on Meta’s advertising measurement so we can see which ads lead to a request. It is off unless you choose Allow, it does not affect your request, and your browser’s Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track setting is respected.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to marketing emails, at any time by emailing hello@frameworkco.ca.
