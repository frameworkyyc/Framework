# Demo websites: photo shot list

The four sample sites at `/demos/` (Fence & Decks, Landscaping, Renovations, General Contractor) have 24 photo slots. Each slot shows a labelled placeholder until its file exists; **the photos below are in place (all 24 from Unsplash, 5 November 2026); to change one, replace the file with the same name and the page picks it up** (no code change). A slot with no file keeps its placeholder, so a partly filled set never shows a broken image.

## Where files go and how to prepare them
- Folder: `assets/img/demos/<demo>/` with names `hero.jpg` and `project-1.jpg` to `project-5.jpg` (JPEG, lower-case).
- Size: hero **1920 x 1080** (landscape; the Landscaping hero is full-width behind the headline, the other three sit in a tall box beside it, so keep the subject near the middle), projects **1200 x 800** (3:2 landscape).
- Weight: hero up to 400 KB, projects up to 250 KB (export at quality ~75, sRGB).
- Content: no readable logos, brand names, licence plates or identifiable faces unless you hold the rights and releases. Photos from Unsplash are fine for commercial use under its licence, which does not cover trademarks or the likeness of identifiable people. Keep a note of each photo's source page.
- The captions in the placeholders describe the shot wanted; they are fictional sample projects, so use photos that fit the description rather than a real client's project.

## Derived images and personalisation
- `assets/img/demos/<demo>/thumb.jpg` (900 x 560) and `tab.jpg` (240 x 150) are small crops used by the showroom at `/demos/` (cards and tabs). They are cut from existing photos (Fence & Decks project-2, Landscaping hero, Renovations hero, General Contractor project-1). If you replace one of those, regenerate the crop so the showroom stays in step. The credits for those photos already cover the crops.
- Visitors can try their own business name, colour and photos on any demo ("Make it yours"). Photos they choose stay in their browser as temporary object URLs; nothing is uploaded or stored.
