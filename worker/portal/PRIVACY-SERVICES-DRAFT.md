# DRAFT: privacy policy additions for Client Services (invoices, support and change requests, contact)

**Status: DRAFT for review. Not published.** `privacy.html` has not been changed. This text needs the owner's review (and ideally an adviser's) before it is added.

## What we collect in Client Services
- **Invoices.** Invoice number, dates, amount (CAD), payment status, an optional description, and the invoice PDF that Framework prepares for you. Framework staff enter these; nothing is read from your payment method, and the portal does not take payments.
- **Support and change requests.** The category, title and description you write, any files you attach (up to three, 10 MB each), the replies between you and Framework, and the status of the request. The conversation is kept as a complete history, including status changes.
- **Contact.** If you email us, we keep the email in our mailbox like any other correspondence.

## Why
To provide, bill for and support Framework services, to respond to your requests, and to keep a record of what was asked, quoted and agreed.

## Requests do not authorize work
Sending a request does not authorize paid work. Where a request involves extra work, Framework will review it and send a quote; work starts only after you approve the quote.

## Who can see it
- Invoices: **owners** of your organization in the portal, and authorized Framework staff.
- Requests and replies: people in your organization who use the portal, and authorized Framework staff. Framework staff may also keep private internal notes on a request; these are not shown to you.
- Attachments and invoice files are stored privately and are only available to your organization and Framework staff.

## Emails
Framework is emailed when you submit or reply to a request. You are emailed when an invoice is published, when Framework replies to a request, or when Framework sends you a quote. Routine status changes and Framework's private notes are not emailed. These emails say that something is waiting in the portal and do not include invoice numbers, amounts or request details. To avoid a flood, a conversation sends at most one reply email to each side every ten minutes.

## How long we keep it
- **Invoices are retained, not routinely deleted.** We keep invoice records for at least six years after the end of the calendar year in which they were issued, to meet bookkeeping and tax record requirements, and archive them from view rather than delete them. After that period they are reviewed before any permanent deletion. *(Confirm the period with an accountant.)*
- **Support requests and attachments** are kept while you are a client and for a limited period afterwards (proposed: 24 months after the request is completed), unless needed for a dispute or a legal obligation. *(Owner to confirm.)*
- If you ask us to delete personal information, we will do so unless we must keep it (for example, invoice records).

## Your choices
Write to hello@frameworkco.ca to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.
