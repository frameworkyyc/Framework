Local SEO for Calgary contractors and trades.

When someone nearby searches for a fence builder, a roofer or a renovator, Google answers with a map and a short list. Local SEO is the work of earning a place on it, and measuring whether it is working.

In plain terms

What local SEO is.

Three things decide whether a local business shows up: your website, your Google Business Profile, and what the rest of the internet says about you. Local SEO is making all three accurate, complete and consistent, so Google can see where you work and what you do.

Nearby communities

Reaching customers in nearby communities.

01

Your profile says where you work

Listing an area on your Google Business Profile tells people where you serve. It does not by itself make you appear there.

02

Your pages show the work

Pages that describe the jobs you do in each place you actually serve give search engines something real to match.

03

Real reviews and time

Reviews from customers in those places, and time, do the rest. We help you build those signals for the communities you serve.

Eligibility

Google Business Profile eligibility.

Google decides which businesses qualify for a profile, and verification is required. Businesses that travel to their customers can usually list the areas they serve instead of a street address.

We check your situation against Google's current guidelines before we start.

What we won't promise

No guarantees.

A ranking, a position on the map or a number of leads. Search results are decided by Google and by your competitors.

We focus on accurate, useful pages and real reviews, not shortcuts, and we measure what happens.

Questions

Questions we get.

How long does local SEO take?

Search results change over weeks and months, not days, and how fast depends on your trade and your competition. We won't promise a date.

Do I need a street address?

Not necessarily. Many businesses that travel to customers list a service area instead of a street address. Google's rules decide what is allowed for your business.

Do I need a new website first?

Not always. The free assessment shows whether your current site helps or hinders.

Can you get me more reviews?

Reviews matter, and they have to come from real customers. They are earned by doing the job well, and by asking.

What does it cost?

We scope the work for your business. The free assessment is the quickest way to see what you would need.
