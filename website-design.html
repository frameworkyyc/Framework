Questions

Questions we get.

What is the difference between design and development?

Design decides how the site looks and how a visitor moves through it. Development builds it so it works, loads quickly and can be changed later. We do both, so nothing is lost between the two.

How long does a website take?

Every project is scoped individually, so we don't publish a standard turnaround. After the first conversation we talk you through the stages and what we need from you.

Will I own my website?

Ownership of your domain and your website is set out in your client agreement, so you know where you stand from the start.

Where is the website hosted?

Where it suits the project, on Cloudflare, the infrastructure Framework itself runs on.

Can you work with the logo and brand I already have?

Yes. Aurora Surveys supplied its own identity and we built the website around it. If you don't have one, branding can be part of the same project.

Will the website get me to the top of Google?

Nobody can promise a ranking, and we won't. We build the technical and on-page foundations search engines look for. Local SEO is the ongoing work on top.

Do you work outside Calgary?

We are based in Calgary and work with businesses in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks and Chestermere. We also work with clients elsewhere in Alberta and across Canada.
